Privacy statement

Last updated 24 November 2026

Tea Craft (“we”, “us”) is a bubble tea and craft café in New Zealand. This statement explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, who sees it, and your rights under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Who to contact

Our privacy officer is the Tea Craft owner. Email teacraftnz@gmail.com for any privacy question, to see or correct your information, or to ask us to delete it.

What we collect and why

WhenWhat we collectWhy
You place an orderName, email, optional mobile number, pickup time, the items you order, the total, and how you chose to pay. For card payments we keep only the last four digits and a payment reference.To make your order, let you know about it, take payment, and keep our business and tax records.
You request a bookingName, email, activity, date, time, number of guests, and any notes you add.To manage and confirm your booking and reply to you.
You join the newsletterYour email address and the date you joined.To send occasional Tea Craft news, only because you asked us to. You can unsubscribe at any time.
You visit the websiteStandard technical information such as your IP address and browser type, handled by our hosting and security provider. We keep your IP address for about a day to stop spam and abuse.To deliver the site securely and prevent misuse.
You build an orderYour drink choices are saved in your own browser (local storage) so your order is still there if you come back. This stays on your device until you place the order.Convenience.

Giving us your details is your choice, but we can’t make an order or booking without the details marked above. We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don’t currently use analytics. If that changes we will update this page first.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your information. We use these service providers to run the website, and they only handle your information for us:

  • Supabase: our database and photo storage (data is held in Sydney, Australia).
  • Cloudflare: website hosting, delivery and security.
  • Stripe: card payments, once online payments are switched on. Your card details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Google (Gmail): the emails we send and receive with you.

Some of these providers store or process information outside New Zealand (for example in Australia and the United States). We choose reputable providers and take reasonable steps to make sure your information is protected to a standard comparable to New Zealand’s. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Orders: up to 7 years, because New Zealand tax and accounting rules require us to keep business records.
  • Booking requests: about 12 months after the booking date.
  • Newsletter emails: until you unsubscribe.
  • Spam-prevention data (IP address): about a day.

Keeping it safe

The site uses HTTPS, our records sit behind a protected login that only authorised Tea Craft staff can use, and we use reputable providers. If a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell the people affected and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. We will respond within 20 working days. You can also ask us to delete your newsletter sign-up or booking details at any time (orders may need to be kept for the tax reason above).

Complaints

Please contact us first so we can put things right. If you are still not happy, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Children

Our online ordering and bookings are meant for adults, or for young people with a parent or caregiver’s help.

Changes

If we change this statement we will update the date at the top.
