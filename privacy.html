Privacy statement

Last updated 24 November 2026

Tea Craft (“we”, “us”) is a bubble tea and craft café in New Zealand. This statement explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, who sees it, and your rights under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Who to contact

Our privacy officer is the Tea Craft owner. Email teacraftnz@gmail.com for any privacy question, to see or correct your information, or to ask us to delete it.

What we collect and why

WhenWhat we collectWhy
You join the newsletterYour email address and the date you joined.To send occasional Tea Craft news, only because you asked us to. You can unsubscribe at any time.
You email usYour email address, name and whatever you tell us.To reply to you.
You visit the websiteStandard technical information such as your IP address and browser type, handled by our hosting and security provider. We keep your IP address for about a day to stop spam and abuse.To deliver the site securely and prevent misuse.

Giving us your details is your choice. We don’t take orders or bookings through this website at the moment, we don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don’t currently use analytics. If any of that changes, we will update this page first.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell your information. We use these service providers to run the website, and they only handle your information for us:

  • Supabase: our database and photo storage (data is held in Sydney, Australia).
  • Cloudflare: website hosting, delivery and security.
  • Google (Gmail): the emails we send and receive with you.

Some of these providers store or process information outside New Zealand (for example in Australia and the United States). We choose reputable providers and take reasonable steps to make sure your information is protected to a standard comparable to New Zealand’s. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Newsletter emails: until you unsubscribe.
  • Emails you send us: for as long as we need them to help you, and for our records.
  • Spam-prevention data (IP address): about a day.

Keeping it safe

The site uses HTTPS, our records sit behind a protected login that only authorised Tea Craft staff can use (with optional two-step verification), and we use reputable providers. If a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell the people affected and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. We will respond within 20 working days. You can ask us to delete your newsletter sign-up at any time, or use our unsubscribe page.

Complaints

Please contact us first so we can put things right. If you are still not happy, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Children

This website is for everyone, but please ask a parent or caregiver before sharing an email address with us.

Changes

If we change this statement we will update the date at the top.
