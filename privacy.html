Privacy

Tea Craft collects only what you send us through our booking form, newsletter sign-up or order email: your name, email address and any notes you add.

How we use it

We use it to confirm bookings, prepare orders and, if you joined the Kawaii club, send occasional news. We don’t sell your details.

Where it’s stored

Booking and newsletter requests arrive as emails in our inbox (teacraftnz@gmail.com). Our website host also keeps standard server logs.

Your choices

Email teacraftnz@gmail.com to see, correct or delete your details, or to leave the newsletter. Your cart is saved only in your own browser.
